NOTE: WHY THE STOCK .spz WAS NOT USED (2026-08-19)
--------------------------------------------------
Idea: since client scenes are under NDA, embed an open-source stock .spz
splat on the reconstruction case study instead.

Checked and rejected for now, on three grounds:

1. LICENSING. The obvious candidates are not cleanly licensed.
   - sparkjs.dev demo assets (butterfly.spz 4.0MB, valley.spz 6.8MB): the
     Spark renderer is MIT, but the demo splats ship with no license file.
   - INRIA / original 3DGS pretrained models: research / non-commercial only.
   - Hugging Face "gaussian splat" datasets: mostly individual uploads with
     self-declared licenses on scenes they may not own.
   Not what you want sitting on a page recruiters read.

2. PAYLOAD. 4-7MB for the splat plus three.js + Spark, on a site whose entire
   built output is 1.2MB. Solvable with click-to-load, but still real.

3. IT PROVES THE WRONG THING. A stock splat demonstrates you can embed
   somebody else's file. It does not demonstrate you built the pipeline.

Better move: the NDA covers client scenes, not the method. Capture a scene
with the iPad app you already built - a stairwell, a park bench, your desk.
That asset is yours, has no licensing question, IS your own pipeline output,
and simultaneously gives the mobile-capture case study its first visual.

If you still want it interactive after that, the build is: Spark (MIT) +
three.js, click-to-load behind the poster frame so it costs nothing until
someone opts in, with a static render as the fallback.
